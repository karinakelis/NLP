Chapter One

Clara Hale worked as a nurse for Dr. Samuel Reed at the town clinic. Dr. Reed was strict, but he respected her. Every evening Clara walked home to her son Oliver.
Oliver Hale was nineteen and restless. His best friend James Ward lived next door. Oliver and James fixed old bicycles together and laughed for hours.

Chapter Two

At the market Oliver met Rosa Vance. She smiled at him, and he could not stop thinking about her. Oliver told James about Rosa, and James teased him.
Dr. Reed accused Clara of losing patient files. Clara denied it, but he shouted at her in front of everyone. She went home and cried.

Chapter Three

James borrowed money from Dr. Reed and never paid it back. Reed threatened James and called him a thief.
Oliver invited Rosa to the river. They danced, and Rosa kissed Oliver for the first time.
Clara did not love her job anymore.

Chapter Four

Dr. Reed found the missing files in his own desk. He apologized to Clara and thanked her for her patience. Clara forgave him.
James repaid the debt, but Reed still did not trust him.
Oliver married Rosa in the spring. Clara hugged her son and welcomed Rosa into the family. James stood beside Oliver at the wedding as his best man.
