Chapter 1

Anna Petrova lived in a small town with her brother Daniel. Anna loved her brother and always helped him with his work. Daniel smiled and thanked Anna for the dinner.
One morning Anna met Mark Ellis at the library. Mark was kind and funny. Anna and Mark talked for hours about old books.
Victor Kane owned the factory where Daniel worked. Victor was a cold and angry man. Victor shouted at Daniel and threatened to fire him.

Chapter 2

Daniel argued with Anna about money. Daniel blamed Anna for their problems and Anna cried. The siblings did not speak for a week.
Mark visited Anna and comforted her. Anna trusted Mark and told him everything.
Victor met Mark in the street. Victor insulted Mark and laughed at him.

Chapter 3

Lucy Kane, the daughter of Victor, became friends with Anna. Lucy and Anna walked in the park every evening. Lucy hated her father's cruelty.
Victor attacked Mark at the factory gate. Mark defended himself, and Lucy helped Mark escape.

Chapter 4

Daniel apologized to Anna. Anna forgave Daniel and hugged him. Daniel and Anna were happy again.
Mark kissed Anna under the old bridge. Anna and Mark were in love and planned to marry.
Lucy left Victor and moved to the city. Victor was alone and bitter.
