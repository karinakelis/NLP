Chapter 1

Mrs. Helen Grant ran a bakery on Elm Street. Her daughter Mia helped her every morning before school. Helen paid a young man named Peter Lowe to deliver bread.
Peter was shy, but Mia liked his jokes. They often shared lunch behind the bakery.
Mr. Arthur Fenn owned the shop across the street. He envied Helen's success.

Chapter 2

Fenn spread rumours that Helen used old flour. Helen confronted him and called him a liar.
Mia asked Peter to help her with a school play. He agreed, and they practised every evening.
Peter's cousin Nora Lowe arrived from the city. Nora disliked Mia at first and mocked her clothes.

Chapter 3

Fenn offered Peter more money to work for him. Peter refused and stayed with Helen.
Nora apologized to Mia. The two girls became close friends and went to the fair together.
At the fair, Peter gave Mia a red ribbon and told her that he loved her.

Chapter 4

The town learned the truth about the rumours, and Fenn closed his shop. He left town without a word.
Helen thanked Peter for his loyalty. Mia and Peter got engaged in the summer.
Nora stayed in town and worked with Helen at the bakery.
